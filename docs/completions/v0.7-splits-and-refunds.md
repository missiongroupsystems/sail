# v0.7 Bill splitting and refunds: completion note

Date: 4 Nov 2026. Branch: `feat/beta-v1`. Roadmap phase 7 (v0.6 → v0.7).

## What

Bill splitting evenly by N, by seat (shared lines split evenly across seats or kept on a separate Shared bill), and by item (whole lines to one bill, or a line shared equally between chosen bills, such as a bottle three ways), free until the first payment and locked per bill afterwards; per-bill receipts, invoices and cash rounding; refunds of a whole bill, of chosen lines and quantities (modifiers follow their parent; every adjustment and GST reversed in proportion) or of an amount spread across the bill's lines; refund tenders assigned to the original payments within per-payment caps, with cash rounding on cash refunds; numbered refund receipts linked to the original bill, printed or sent digitally; numbered credit notes (PDF) when the bill was invoiced; Manager/Finance/Admin or a lower role with a manager override, reason required; refunds dated by the business day they happen and counted in shift close and the Z-report; refunded items do not restore daily caps.

## How

- **Splits are share vectors.** Every mode computes one share vector per line and calls `syncAllocations`, so the allocator in `shared` is the only place an amount is divided; each component is split separately and a bill's total is the sum of its components, which is why bills add up to the order to the cent (asserted in the database at commit). Ties go to earlier bills.
- **Locking.** Any split or move touching a paid bill's lines is refused (`check_locked_by_payment`); lines on unpaid bills can still move between unpaid bills.
- **Refunds read allocations, never re-price.** `refundableFor` builds, per line on the bill, its allocated components and what earlier refunds already took; `quoteRefund` reverses in proportion (quantity/line quantity, or amount/remaining total), clamps every component to what is left, lets the base absorb rounding so parts add up exactly, and splits fee and discount reversals across the line's adjustments. Tenders default to a proportional split across payments by what each can still return.
- **Database caps:** deferred triggers assert that a refund's lines and tenders both equal its total, that a line's refunds on a bill never exceed its allocation, and that a payment's refunds never exceed what it paid. Refund rows are immutable.
- **Documents:** refund receipt number (`RR-`) always; credit note (`CN-`) when the bill had an invoice, rendered from the stored credit-note document with "Against invoice INV-…" and the reason. Both numbers come from the gap-free sequences.
- **Cash:** cash refunds carry `rounding_cents`; shift close and the Z-report subtract `cash_out` from expected cash.

## Why

Bills and refunds are where cent drift and over-refunding would surface; both read only the pipeline's per-line amounts and go through the one allocator, so the invariants the scope doc demands (checks add up to the order; refunds never exceed what was paid) hold by construction and are re-checked by Postgres.

## Decisions recorded

- Shares are equal fractions only (1/N), as the scope doc fixes for v0.9; uneven fractions stay deferred.
- Goodwill refunds (by amount) record quantity 0 on their lines so items are not treated as returned.
- The invoice and credit-note PDFs use the built-in Helvetica (WinAnsi): the true minus sign becomes a hyphen and non-Latin names a placeholder; embedding a CJK font is a follow-up for outlets that need it.
