# v0.6 Payments, shifts and tax documents: completion note

Date: 4 Nov 2026. Branch: `feat/beta-v1`. Roadmap phase 6 (v0.5 → v0.6).

## What

Checks (bills) and allocations with the model that v0.7 splits use; payments with several tenders per bill and part payments until the balance is zero; card, PayNow, e-wallet and house-account tenders recorded by hand with an optional reference; cash rounding on the settling cash tender only, stored as `rounding_cents` and never touching tax or line amounts; cash tips; the pricing freeze at the first payment with lines added afterwards landing on a new bill priced with line-level rules only; "pay before firing" stations; order completion and the table statuses "bill requested" and "paid/free"; reopen on the same business day (Manager+) reversing payments as records; printed pre-bills and receipts through the relay with the shared bill template; digital receipts by public token (QR/link now, email/SMS once a provider exists); numbered PDF tax invoices with the outlet UEN and gap-free numbering; shifts with a counted float and counted cash, expected vs counted including rounding, variance with a reason; business-day close; and the basic Z-report per outlet or station.

## How

- **Allocations exist once.** `syncAllocations` runs after every re-price: unlocked bills are rebuilt from the current line components with `allocateLine` (each component split by largest remainder, bill total = sum of its components), so bills always add up to the order; the database asserts both sums at commit. A bill with a payment is locked and its allocations never change; a line on a locked bill cannot be edited or voided (`check_locked_by_payment`).
- **Payments** apply to a bill's balance. Non-cash cannot exceed the balance. For cash, an amount above the balance is the cash tendered; when a cash tender settles the balance and the merchant has rounding on, `roundCash` gives `rounding_cents` and change is `tendered − (amount + rounding)`. Payments are append-only (`payments_no_delete`); `paid_total ≤ total` is a CHECK.
- **Freeze:** the first payment stores `freezeFrom(result, rules)` on the order and re-prices under the frozen context, so stored adjustments carry `frozen: true`; order-level manual rules are then refused (`pricing_frozen`), the type cannot change, and new lines go to a new bill.
- **Documents:** `nextDocumentNo` is one `INSERT … ON CONFLICT … RETURNING` under the row lock, so five concurrent invoices get INV-000001..000005 with no gap (tested). The invoice stores the bill document at issue time; the PDF is rendered from that snapshot, so reprints are identical.
- **Shifts:** payments attach to the open shift of their station (or the outlet-wide shift). Close computes expected cash = float + cash payments + rounding − cash refunds (v0.7 provider) and the variance; the Z figures are stored on the shift. Business-day close requires every shift closed and snapshots the Z-report.
- **Z-report** reads orders, adjustments, payments and voided lines by business date; refunds plug in through `registerRefundTotals` in v0.7; the full rule/reporting-class breakdown and materialised views arrive in v0.9.

## Why

Money must be recorded exactly once, against an exact balance, with every rounding cent accounted for (scope §4.3, §6). Building bills and allocations now, with the invariants in the database, means v0.7's splits and refunds only add shares and reversals on top of a model that already balances.

## Blocked inputs

- Email/SMS provider for digital receipts (roadmap §6): the link and QR work; email/SMS record `provider_not_configured` and the API returns a warning.
- Confirmation of the required tax-invoice content (Finance): the PDF shows merchant, outlet, address, UEN, number, date, order and bill, lines, adjustments, GST line, total, payments and footer.

## Decisions recorded

- New lines after the first payment open a new bill instead of changing the paid one.
- Reopen clears the pricing freeze and unlocks bills (the order returns to its pre-payment state); payments are reversed records, never deleted.
- Order completion happens automatically when the last bill is paid and every item is sent, or explicitly through `orders.complete`.
- `payments.void` is still not built (vision open question 1); wrong tenders are corrected by reopen (same day) or refund (v0.7).
